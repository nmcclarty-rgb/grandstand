Blind Five

  1. An attribute is drawn: goals, assists, goal involvements, appearances or discipline. A series is a best of three, a different attribute each match.
  2. Draft a fresh team for it (five or eleven, from a rolled formation) from 34 seasons of Premier League players, on a budget. Each round eight players of one position go on the board with a name and a price: no stats, no club, nothing else. You and the bot take turns to pick one.
  3. Add it up. Each team's real career totals in the attribute; the bigger total wins the match. First to two wins.

A price is a hint, not the truth. Know your football.
