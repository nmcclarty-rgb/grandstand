Prem Wheel

  1. A wheel picks the attribute for the match: goals, assists, goal involvements, appearances, league titles, wins, clean sheets or discipline.
  2. Draft a fresh team for it (five or eleven, in a rolled formation) from 34 seasons of Premier League players, on a budget. Each round eight players of one position go on the board with a name and a price: no stats, no club, nothing else. You and the bot take turns to pick one.
  3. Add it up. Each team's real career totals in the attribute; the bigger total wins the match.

A price is a hint, not the truth. Know your football.
